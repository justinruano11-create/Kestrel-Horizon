What we do

Buy it right. Make it better. Keep it.

Three ways we put capital to work, all in the same markets.

01

Value-add acquisitions

We buy 1–4 unit homes and small multifamily buildings priced below what they are worth once the work is done.

02

Ground-up spec homes

On infill and waterfront lots, we build new homes from the ground up, where the land is worth more than what stands on it today.

03

Long-term hold

We renovate, rent and refinance, then keep the property. We buy to own it for years, not to pass it along.

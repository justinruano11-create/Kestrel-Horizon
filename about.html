About

Hard assets. Long view.

Kestrel Horizon is a South Florida residential real estate investment company. We buy property that needs work, do the work and keep what we improve.

The name

Watch first. Then move.

A kestrel is a small falcon that hovers in place, studies the ground and only then commits. That is how we buy.

Horizon is where we keep our eyes. We buy to own for years, so every decision is made for the long term.

How we operate

Three rules we don't bend.

Numbers first

We underwrite before we get excited. Price, budget, rents and refinance value decide the deal.

Do the work

Value comes from the renovation and the management, not from waiting on the market.

Own it for years

We buy to hold. That keeps us careful about what we buy and how we fix it.
